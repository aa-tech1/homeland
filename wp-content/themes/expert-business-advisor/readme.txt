== Description ==

The Expert Business Advisor WordPress Theme is specifically designed for professionals in the consulting industry, offering a robust platform for business consultants, startup advisors, and advisory firms. Tailored to meet the needs of business coaches and development consultants, this theme provides a clean, modern design that effectively showcases your expertise and services. It serves various purposes, from displaying business advisory services and strategies to promoting coaching programs and startup mentorship. This theme features customizable layouts, allowing consultants to create dedicated pages for their services, client testimonials, and case studies, emphasising their unique selling propositions. With an emphasis on professional aesthetics, the high-resolution visuals and intuitive navigation ensure potential clients can easily access crucial information, such as startup funding strategies and business growth consultations. Moreover, the theme incorporates integrated call-to-action buttons that encourage visitors to engage, whether they&#8217;re searching for a “business consultant near me” or in need of tailored startup guidance. Its mobile-friendly and SEO-optimised design not only enhances visibility but also aids consultants in connecting with a broader audience. The Expert Business Advisor WordPress Theme not only elevates your online presence but also positions you as a trusted authority in the consulting space, facilitating your business&#8217;s growth and transformation.

== Installation ==
	
1. In your admin panel, go to Appearance > Themes and click the Add New button.
2. Click Upload and Choose File, then select the theme's .zip file. Click Install Now.
3. Click Activate to use your new theme right away.
